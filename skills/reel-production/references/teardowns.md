## Patterns across all of them

- One idea, one device, one accent colour. The look is chosen for the brand and held, not defaulted (dark is not automatically premium: ElevenLabs is high-key).
- Frame 0 is already moving; the hook reads in under a second.
- Salient sound hits sit near 1 per second in every narrated reference; texture underneath can be dense. Cuts and visual events land on sound.
- The voice, when there is one, is a single calm continuous read at 2.2-3.1 words/s; picture and type lock to its words.
- Endings return: the opening lockup, a loop, or the brand handoff.
- Real detail everywhere the camera looks: real UI copy, bevels, engraving, textures; placeholders and primitives read as fake instantly.
